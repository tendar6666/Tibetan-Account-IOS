Privacy Policy / གསང་རྒྱའི་སྲིད་ཇུས།

Last updated: October 2026

Tibetan Account Book (བོད་ཀྱི་རྩིས་དེབ།) is an offline-first bookkeeping and cashbook application designed for Tibetan community organizations, small businesses, monasteries, and individuals. Your privacy is our highest priority.

1. Data Collection & Storage

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal financial data. All books, accounts, transactions, party ledgers, financial vouchers, and receipt photos created in Tibetan Account Book are stored strictly locally on your device inside the application's private, sandboxed on-device database (localStorage and IndexedDB).

2. No Account or Registration Required

The application requires no sign-up, user accounts, passwords, or personal identity verification. You can use all features 100% anonymously and offline.

3. Cloud Sync (Optional)

Cloud Sync is completely optional and disabled by default. If you choose to configure a private cloud endpoint (such as your own Firebase Realtime Database), data is transmitted directly and solely to your designated endpoint using your private key. We have no access to your cloud keys or synced databases.

4. Third-Party Services & Fonts

The application loads open-source Tibetan typography fonts via Google Fonts (Noto Sans Tibetan) to ensure proper Tibetan script rendering. No personal financial information is ever shared with font providers.

5. Data Backup and Export

You maintain complete ownership and control of your financial records. You can export a full backup file (.json) or bilingual spreadsheet (.csv) to your phone's local Files storage at any time, or permanently clear all local data using the built-in reset feature.

6. Non-Lending Disclaimer

Tibetan Account Book is purely an informational digital ledger and bookkeeping utility. It is not a financial institution, bank, or loan provider, and does not disburse or broker loans.

7. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via GitHub.
